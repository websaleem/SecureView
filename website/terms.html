2. Description of the Extension

SecureView is a Chrome browser extension that tracks your active browsing time per domain, categorizes visited websites, and presents daily usage summaries. It is designed as a personal productivity and self-awareness tool. Your browsing records are stored locally on your device. Sites that the built-in rule set cannot classify are sent to a cloud AI classification service running on Amazon Web Services — this happens automatically and is not something you opt in to; the "Force AI Classification" setting extends it to every site. Only the URL, hostname and page title are sent (see our Privacy Policy).

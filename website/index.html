Why Choose SecureView?

Intelligent Categorization

SecureView automatically groups your browsing into 11 intuitive categories — Technology, Social Media, Finance, Entertainment, and more — using a built-in rule set backed by AI classification for unrecognized sites.

Active Time Tracking

Only counts time when you're actually browsing. Idle detection and window focus tracking ensure your stats reflect real usage, not time spent away from your desk.

Privacy by Design

All browsing data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API. You control what is tracked — exclude any domain instantly with one click and clear your data at any time.

How SecureView Works

SecureView uses a two-stage categorization approach. A built-in rule set covers thousands of popular domains instantly — no network call needed. For sites not in the rule set, an AI classification service powered by Amazon Bedrock determines the category.

The AI pipeline runs on AWS infrastructure (CloudFront → Lambda@Edge → API Gateway → Lambda → Bedrock). Only the site URL, hostname, and page title are ever sent — and results are cached locally so the same domain is never sent twice.

  • Local-First: Rule-based matching needs no internet connection.
  • Local Storage: Daily browsing records stay on your device in Chrome's storage API.
  • You're in Control: Exclude domains, toggle AI classification, or clear all data from the popup.
  • Transparent: No analytics engines, no selling of data, no third-party trackers.
